Demos for IGC-DPMNet

Introduction

Target speaker extraction (TSE) aims to extract the speech of a designated target speaker from a noisy or multi-speaker mixture. Despite recent advances in extraction performance, state-of-the-art TSE models often incur substantial model sizes and computational costs, posing significant challenges for resource-constrained scenarios. In this study, we propose IGC-DPMNet, a lightweight TSE network designed to achieve a favorable trade-off between extraction performance and compu- tational complexity. Specifically, we introduce a Two-stage Target Conditioning framework incorporating an Interactive Guidance Calibration (IGC) module, which leverages global enrollment representations to dynamically calibrate frame-level interactive guidance for accurate target speaker conditioning. Within the TSE backbone, we develop an Interleaved Dual-Path Modeling Separator (InterDPMS). By alternately integrating BLSTM- based dual-path blocks and Mamba-based dual-path modeling blocks, InterDPMS exploits complementary heterogeneous long- range sequence modeling to maximize representational capacity while substantially reducing model complexity and computational cost. Furthermore, a Fine-Grained TF Refinement (FG-TFR) block is embedded into a lightweight decoder to refine local time-frequency representations and suppress feature artifacts for high-fidelity speech reconstruction. Extensive experiments across diverse datasets and acoustic conditions, including Libri2Mix, WSJ0-2Mix, WHAM!, and WHAMR!, demonstrate the robust generalization and effectiveness of the proposed approach. No- tably, IGC-DPMNet achieves competitive performance with only 1.99M parameters and 13.45G MACs, highlighting its strong potential for effective and computationally efficient TSE tasks.

The work presented on this page is derived from a paper, which has been submitted to TASLP, titled 'IGC-DPMNet: Interactive Guidance Calibration and Interleaved Dual-Path Modeling for Efficient Target Speaker Extraction'.
